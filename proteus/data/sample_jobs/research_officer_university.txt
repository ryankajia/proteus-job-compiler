Research Officer — Environmental Monitoring Program
at a large Australian university

The Institute for Environmental Science is seeking a Research Officer to support a
multi-site soil and water monitoring program. You will coordinate field sampling
rounds, manage the resulting datasets, run the statistical analyses, and prepare
reports for the funding body and partner agencies.

Classification: Officer 6/7. Salary $100,000 - $115,000 plus 17% superannuation.
Full time, fixed term for three years.
Closes: 30 November 2026

Selection criteria:
- A degree in environmental science, statistics or a related field, or an
  equivalent combination of relevant experience and education/ training.
- Demonstrated experience managing and analysing large datasets, with proficiency
  in Python or R.
- Proven organisational skills and the ability to prioritise competing deadlines
  across concurrent projects.
- Demonstrated high-level written communication, including experience producing
  reports for external stakeholders.
- Experience coordinating work across multiple sites or institutions.
- Demonstrated understanding of work health and safety requirements in a
  laboratory or field setting.

Applicants must have the right to work in Australia.
